Ryan Decker

Ryan Decker (b. 1997) is a sculptor and furniture designer from Hendersonville, North Carolina. His work tiptoes between abstraction and representation, using the familiarity of functional objects to reexamine our relationship to the natural and built environment. Decker’s first solo furniture show opened in May 2022 at Superhouse Gallery (NY), and his first solo sculpture show opened in April 2024 at Blade Study (NY). He has also exhibited with FTW, R & Company, New Image Art, Alcova, Emma Scully Gallery, and Jonald Dudd. Decker was a 2023 Pioneer Works resident artist and lives and works in Hudson, New York.
